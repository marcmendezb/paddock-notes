Max Verstappen won the Mexico City Grand Prix by 0.799 s from Lewis Hamilton. Andrea Kimi Antonelli was third and, 119 points clear with 100 still available, is already world champion.

## The result

Hamilton started from pole, but Charles Leclerc led at the end of the first lap from third on the grid. Verstappen, who started fifth, was second by the end of that lap.

The podium was Verstappen, Hamilton and Antonelli. Leclerc finished fourth and George Russell, who started second, sixth.

Russell's race went wrong at the start. He lost three places on the first lap and spent twelve laps in fifth without gaining ground.

## The key moment: lap 43

<StrategyFigure season={2026} round={19} caption="Every driver's strategy. Shaded bands mark the VSC on laps 7 and 8, the safety car on lap 44, the red flag on lap 45 and the safety car on laps 59 to 62." />

Leclerc and Verstappen stopped on lap 27. Antonelli, on softs from the start, stayed out and led from lap 28 to lap 43.

Leading didn't mean being faster. Between laps 28 and 43, on an increasingly worn soft, Antonelli's median was 1:21.315. Verstappen, on fresh softs, lapped in 1:20.780: half a second a lap quicker. Extending the first stint gave him the lead on track, but the stop was still to come.

He finally stopped on lap 43 and fitted mediums. The safety car came out on lap 44 and the red flag on lap 45. With the race stopped, teams can change tyres without losing places: almost everyone at the front did. Antonelli had already paid for his stop and dropped to fourth. Verstappen led from lap 44 to the flag.

## What TV didn't show

<PaceFigure season={2026} round={19} drivers={[3, 44]} caption="Lap times for Verstappen and Hamilton. Pit, out and neutralised laps, and the restart after the red flag, are hidden." />

After the red flag, Verstappen and Hamilton lapped at almost the same pace. Between laps 48 and 58, Verstappen's median was 1:19.809 and Hamilton's 1:19.862: +0.053 s per lap. Lap 46 was the stop and lap 47 the restart.

The safety car on laps 59 to 62 bunched the field. Over the last nine laps the pace difference was just +0.033 s per lap. Hamilton set the fastest lap, 1:19.268 on lap 50, but never found a way to attack.

Leclerc, second after the red flag, was slower in that last stint: a 1:20.373 median between laps 48 and 58, +0.564 s per lap off Verstappen. That's why he finished fourth.

Liam Lawson started last and crossed the line ninth. The official classification has him seventh: Oscar Piastri and Franco Colapinto crossed the line ahead of him, but are listed 13th and 15th after post-race penalties. This data doesn't tell us what penalty each one received.

## What changes in the championship

Antonelli has 380 points. Verstappen has 261 and Hamilton 259. Four Grands Prix remain, Brazil, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi, none with a sprint: at most 100 more points each.

At 119 points clear, nobody can catch Antonelli. He is the 2026 world champion. What's left is the fight for second: Verstappen, Hamilton and Russell are covered by 11 points.

## The race in three numbers

- **43 laps** on the same set of softs: Antonelli's first stint, the longest among the front-runners.
- **28 laps** in the lead for Verstappen, from lap 44 to 71, after starting fifth.
- **15 places** gained by Lawson between the grid and the official classification, the biggest climb of the day.
