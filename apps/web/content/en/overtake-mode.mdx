Since 2026 DRS no longer exists: a car within one second of the car ahead gets extra electrical energy to overtake, anywhere on the track.

## Overtake mode

Under the 2026 rules the electric motor plays a much bigger role. A driver within one second of the car ahead at the detection point can use extra energy, called overtake mode, to attempt a pass. Every driver also has Boost, which deploys the engine and battery's maximum power.

## What DRS was

From 2011 to 2025, DRS opened a slot in the rear wing in fixed zones on the straights. Less drag meant more top speed for the car following another.

## What changes

- DRS only worked in marked zones; overtake mode can be used anywhere.
- DRS acted on aerodynamics; overtake mode acts on energy.

These rules are new and may be adjusted, so we review them at the start of every season.
