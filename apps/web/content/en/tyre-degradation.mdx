Tyres degrade because wear and temperature reduce their grip lap after lap; in lap times it shows as a rising slope.

## The idea

Every lap the tyre loses some rubber and works at temperatures that tire it out. With less grip, the driver brakes earlier and gets on the power later. Losing 0.1 s a lap sounds small, but over 20 laps it adds up to 2 seconds.

## How tyres degrade

- **Wear:** the rubber is used up until little is left.
- **Graining:** rubber pellets on the surface when the tyre slides, especially in the cold.
- **Blistering:** blisters from overheating inside the rubber.
- **Cliff:** the moment the tyre suddenly stops working and lap times jump by seconds.
