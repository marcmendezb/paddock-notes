A telemetry chart shows how the car behaves along the lap (speed, throttle and brake); comparing two drivers shows where each one gains time.

## The idea

The horizontal axis is the distance covered in the lap. Each corner appears as a dip in speed, with the brake applied just before and the throttle going back to 100% on the exit.

## How to read it

- **Speed:** who is faster on the straights and who carries more speed through each corner.
- **Brake:** who brakes later; in our data it's an on/off signal.
- **Throttle:** who gets back to full throttle first.
- **Delta:** the accumulated difference. When the curve rises, the second driver is losing time in that stretch.

## A real example

At Mexico 2026, Lewis Hamilton's fastest lap was 0.743 s quicker than Max Verstappen's best, with the biggest difference between metre 3,240 and 3,740.

## How to spot it in our charts

Open the [telemetry comparison](/en/analysis/2026/19/telemetry), hover or use the arrow keys. The track map colours each segment by who was faster.
