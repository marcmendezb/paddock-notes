The sprint format adds a short Saturday race, with its own qualifying, that awards points to the top eight.

## The idea

At some Grands Prix the weekend changes. There's only one practice session; then come sprint qualifying, the sprint itself and, finally, Grand Prix qualifying and Sunday's race.

## The points

Since 2022 the sprint pays 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 points to the top eight. In 2021, its first year, only the top three scored.

## What changes for teams

With less practice, there's less time to tune the car before it goes into parc fermé. Parc fermé rules on sprint weekends change often, so we review them at the start of every season.

## How to spot it in our charts

In the [calendar](/en/races) sprint rounds carry a "Sprint" tag, and their Grand Prix page shows the sprint result separately.
