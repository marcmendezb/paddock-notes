Since 2010 the top ten score 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 and 1 points; the system has changed several times since 1950.

## The current system

The winner takes 25 points and tenth place takes 1. On sprint weekends the sprint points are added (8 for the winner). The fastest lap earned a bonus point from 2019 to 2024 and stopped doing so in 2025.

## How it has changed

| Seasons | Points for the winner | Scoring places |
|---|---|---|
| 1950–1959 | 8 (+1 for fastest lap) | Top 5 |
| 1961–1990 | 9 | Top 6 |
| 1991–2002 | 10 | Top 6 |
| 2003–2009 | 10 | Top 8 |
| Since 2010 | 25 | Top 10 |

For decades, only each driver's best results counted, and the rest were dropped.

## That's why we compare with care

Adding up points from different eras doesn't compare drivers fairly. In [head to head](/en/compare) we also show per-race rates.
