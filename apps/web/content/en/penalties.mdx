Penalties range from a few seconds added to the race time to places lost on the grid; the FIA stewards decide them.

## The most common ones

| Penalty | What it means |
|---|---|
| 5 or 10 seconds | Served stationary at the next stop, or added to the final time |
| Drive-through | Going through the pit lane without stopping |
| Stop-and-go | Stopping for 10 seconds in the pits with no work on the car |
| Grid penalty | Starting further back in the same or the next race |
| Penalty points | Reaching 12 within 12 months means a race ban |

## Who decides

The stewards investigate each incident and publish their decision. Some come after the race and change the result, which is why we review the latest results every week.
