Qualifying has three phases (Q1, Q2 and Q3): the slowest drivers drop out in the first two, and the last ten fight for pole in Q3.

## The idea

Each phase is a timed session where each driver's best lap counts. At the end of Q1 and Q2 the slowest drivers are knocked out and fill the back of the grid. Ten drivers reach Q3, and the fastest starts from pole position.

## Why it matters so much

Overtaking is hard. Starting ahead lets you race in clean air and choose your strategy. A tenth in qualifying can be worth more than several in the race.

## Good to know

- The final grid can change because of penalties, for example for replacing engine parts.
- To start the race, a driver must set a Q1 time within 107% of the fastest, unless the stewards allow otherwise.

## How to spot it in our charts

On every [Grand Prix page](/en/races), the qualifying table shows each driver's Q1, Q2 and Q3 times.
