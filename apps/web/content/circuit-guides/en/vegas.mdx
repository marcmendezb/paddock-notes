## Las Vegas in one sentence

A fast, cold, night-time street circuit laid out on city roads, with a long straight down the Strip, the boulevard of casinos.

The race is held on Saturday night, local time.

## Key corners

- **The first corner.** After the start straight, a heavy stop into a left-hander with the track still cold in the first laps. At the start, it's the first place to fight.
- **The Sphere section.** A sequence of corners around the spherical building. It's slow and tight, with the walls very close.
- **The chicane at the end of the Strip.** After the longest straight of the lap, the heaviest braking zone. It's the best place to overtake, with the slipstream of the whole boulevard.

## What kind of car it suits

One that gains time on the straights without losing it in the slow corners. Teams run low-downforce wings to go faster down the Strip, which makes the cars slide in the slow section.

## Strategy and temperature

November nights in the desert are cold, and so is the asphalt. Tyres struggle to reach their working temperature: when they're cold they grip less and wear unevenly. Warming them properly on the out-lap matters as much as when to stop.

Every driver's strategy in each race since 2023, lap by lap, is in the [technical zone](/en/analysis).

## A moment in history

2024. George Russell won the race and Max Verstappen, fifth, clinched his fourth world title there with two races to go.
