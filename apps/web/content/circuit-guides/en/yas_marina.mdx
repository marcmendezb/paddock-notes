## Yas Marina in one sentence

The season finale: it starts at dusk and ends at night, with two long straights and a slow final section between the hotel and the marina.

The layout was changed in 2021 to make the corners faster and overtaking easier.

## Key corners

- **The hairpin that opens the long straight.** A slow corner: a good exit decides your speed all the way down the straight.
- **The chicane at the end of the long straight.** The heaviest braking zone of the lap and the main overtaking spot, with the slipstream of the whole straight.
- **The banked hairpin after the second straight.** Added in the 2021 changes, it's the second chance to pass on the same lap.
- **The final sector.** Slow, consecutive corners by the hotel, where passing is hard.

## What kind of car it suits

A balanced one: it needs top speed for the straights and traction out of the slow final corners.

## Strategy and temperature

The race starts in daylight and finishes on a cooler track. As the temperature drops, the tyres behave differently at the end than at the start. Every driver's strategy in each race since 2023 is in the [technical zone](/en/analysis).

## A moment in history

2021. Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton arrived level on points at the final race. After a safety car in the closing laps, Verstappen passed Hamilton on the last lap and won his first title.
