## Losail in one sentence

A circuit built for motorbike racing, raced at night in the desert, with a single long straight and almost everything else made of fast, linked corners.

## Key corners

- **Turn 1.** At the end of the pit straight, the heaviest braking zone of the lap. It's practically the only clear overtaking spot.
- **The fast middle section.** A sequence of high-speed corners where the car is always loaded up. That's where the lap is won, but following closely is hard: dirty air takes away downforce exactly where it's needed most.
- **The final corner.** A long, fast right-hander onto the straight. A good exit here is what makes a move into turn 1 possible.

## What kind of car it suits

One with plenty of downforce that's stable in fast corners. With so little straight, trading downforce for top speed doesn't pay off much.

## Strategy and tyres

The fast corners and the kerbs are very hard on tyres. In 2023, after tyre damage linked to the kerbs was found, the number of laps each set could run was capped: every team had to stop three times. Every driver's strategy in each race since 2023 is in the [technical zone](/en/analysis).

The race is at night, but heat and humidity stay high. In 2023 several drivers finished with physical problems.

## A moment in history

2021, Formula 1's first visit to Qatar. Lewis Hamilton won from pole in the middle of his title fight with Max Verstappen.
